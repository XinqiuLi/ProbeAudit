Retained extension design recorded 5 October 2026

The original protocol was recorded before selection and generation of the
additional sequence results. Findings from the historical cases were already
known. The complete original protocol is preserved in the version 1.0.0
archive, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23273760. This release extracts the scientific
sampling and matching design, omitting historical task-delivery instructions.

Eligible GPL570 sets have exactly one distinct platform symbol with
gene_biotype:lncRNA in retained Ensembl 116 noncoding FASTA headers, exactly
eleven complete ACGT 25-mers and unique X/Y array coordinates. Other platform
symbols remain in the annotation. The historical fourteen are excluded.
Ascending SHA-256 of 'ProbeAudit-R6-20261005|' plus probe ID defines the
ordering; ID breaks ties and the first 100 are retained. Expression, response,
model performance, RefSeq matches and categories do not enter selection.

The retained sequence engine evaluates original sequences and reverse
complements against Ensembl 116 coding and noncoding references and RefSeq
RS_2025_08 RNA, with exact matches and at most one substitution kept separate.
The genomic search is exact, contiguous and both-stranded against the GRCh38
primary assembly. Target absence is recorded separately from lack of matches
to a represented target. The A/B/C reporting conditions are the same in both
references. The original probe IDs and complete platform labels are retained.
